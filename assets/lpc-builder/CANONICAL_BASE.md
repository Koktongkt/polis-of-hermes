# Canonical LPC Community Base

`sources/image_c76574.png` is the approved starting environment and visual reference for future Polis expansion.

## Rules

- Preserve the complete 1001×817 image without repainting, cropping, stretching, or moving its buildings.
- Characters remain independent runtime entities; none are baked into the base.
- Expand outward through the existing north, east, and west road contacts, or into the east/south grass seams.
- Match the base perspective, pixel density, upper-left lighting, grass/earth/cobble materials, building scale, and prop density.
- Additions should use source assets or separately approved art and must not overwrite the immutable core.
- The source/license is currently unknown, so this remains review-only and must not be published until provenance is resolved.

See `canonical-base.json` for hashes, protected features, and expansion seams.
